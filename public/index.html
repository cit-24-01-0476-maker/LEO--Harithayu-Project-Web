❧THE STORY OF HARITHAYU

A shelter for the coast. A new space for life.

Mangroves are more than trees at the water's edge. They shelter young marine life, help protect coastlines and sustain an ecosystem worth caring for today.

Harithayu by the Leo Club of SLTC aims to support the planting of 500 mangrove seedlings in a suitable habitat in Mannar. Your LKR 100 contribution can be part of that journey.
